## 4/9

Freshwater science is a major center of gravity.

Lakes 28% · Microbial 18% · Biogeochem 17% · Climate as forcing 10% · Rivers 9% · Marine 9%.

About 93% of talks carry ≥1 of those tags. Freshwater systems (lake+river+wetland 39%) outweigh saltwater (15%).

(4/9)

## 5/9

Indigenous-knowledge, equity, community-led, and citizen-science framings: 58 talks, 14 session items, about 4%.

EP013 "Two-Eyed Seeing" programmed as a science session. WS05, WS08 as dedicated workshops. AV001 features ECRs from the Global South.
