## Summary in one paragraph

ASLO-SIL 2026 is a freshwater-and-microbial conference held in a North American city, structured around a synthesis push ("bridging gaps," "multiple stressors," "convergence"). Limnology and biogeochemistry are the two largest tracks; eDNA and AI/ML are visible but not yet dominant; FT-ICR-MS DOM work is essentially a niche of two talks in 1,462. Indigenous-led and equity-framed work is intentionally scaffolded as a cross-cut. Erika's talk sits in one of the smallest sub-communities of the meeting (DOM chemistry, ~3%) but uses exactly the integrative framing the rest of the schedule is reaching for — which is the right way to be small and remembered rather than small and ignored.
