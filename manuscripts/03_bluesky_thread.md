**Format:** 9 posts, each ≤280 chars. Post 1/9 has the hook + data point. Last post has the close + soft CTA (link to the L&O Bulletin piece / GitHub repo / longer essay).

## 1/9

1,455 presentations. 308 sessions. ~1,400 presenters. ~740 institutions. 5 days in Montreal.

I read the full ASLO-SIL 2026 program before the meeting. Here is what it tells us about ourselves as a community.

🧵 (1/9)

---

## 2/9

The verbs have changed.

2006 session names said *measure, describe, characterise.*
2026 says *bridge, integrate, converge, scale.*

"Multiple stressors" appears 9× as a bigram. "Bridging the gap" 8×. "Towards convergence" 6×.

This is a field naming its synthesis moment.

(2/9)

---

## 3/9

Three giants honored simultaneously: Mike Pace, Jim Cotner, Jim Elser.

Three legacy sessions in one meeting is unusual. It tells us where the community sits in its life cycle.

(3/9)

---

## 4/9

But — and this is the part to notice —

the legacy honoring is paired *structurally* with early-career scaffolding.

4 sessions of Amplifying Voices.
An ECR resilience workshop.
A cross-organisational ECR alliance.
A "How To" session for first-timers.

The field is handing off in public.

(4/9)

---

## 5/9

The SIL half is doing real work.

Lakes & limnology = 28% of the program.
Rivers + wetlands = another 11%.
Marine oceanography = 9%.

This is not the marine-heavy ASLO of 2010. The joint meeting + Montreal venue has put freshwater science back in the front of the room.

(5/9)

---

## 6/9

The inclusivity scaffold is structural, not decorative.

"Two-Eyed Seeing" programs Indigenous knowledge as a *knowledge system.*
Amplifying Voices foregrounds ECRs from the Philippines, Kenya, Argentina, Brazil — regions underrepresented in a corpus where US+Canada = 54%.

(6/9)

---

## 7/9

The bets being placed are also legible.

AI / ML: 35 talks (~2%) — modest but pointed.
eDNA / -omics: 47 talks (~3%) — undertold vs ESA / AGU.
Long-term monitoring: 9% — the field's methodological soul.
FT-ICR-MS on DOM: 2 talks. (Disclosure: one of them is mine.)

(7/9)

---

## 8/9

The 2026 portrait:

Handing off its giants.
Scaffolding its cohort.
Programming Indigenous knowledge as knowledge.
Betting modestly on AI and -omics.
Reaffirming long-term observation as identity.

Verbs shifted from describe → integrate. The bets placed this week will look obvious in 2031.

(8/9)
